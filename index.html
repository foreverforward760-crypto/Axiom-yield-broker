Fraudulent MC pattern screening — Operating Pattern Analysis algorithm
How the detection algorithm works

Legitimate carriers build operational history through natural growth cycles — periods of high volume followed by capacity contraction, route changes, and recovery. This creates measurable variance. Fraudulent carriers show none of it. Their MC history is artificially clean: recently issued authority, zero load volume variance, and suspiciously high contact responsiveness across every lane. The Ghost Filter scores the absence of authentic operational variance as the primary fraud signal.

Ghost Score = (authority_age × 0.30) + (volume_variance × 0.40) + (contact_anomaly × 0.30)
authority_age = min(carrier_age_yrs × 20, 100) · volume_variance = normalized load history variance
contact_anomaly = (10 − contact_quality) × 10 · Flag threshold: score < 35 → Ghost pattern confirmed
Primary signal: A carrier available on every lane, answering every call on the first ring, with no capacity constraints — is a red flag, not a green one. Unlimited availability = synthetic operation. Legitimate owner-operators have defined capacity windows.

The Blind Spot Metric — why perfect scorecards mask real risk
High Asset Density — Even stages (2, 4, 6, 8)
Fleet capacity is present and bookable. HOS banked. Equipment available. Surface metrics look strong. Stage 4 is optimal. Stage 8 is the trap — identical surface profile, opposite failure risk.
Constrained Capacity — Odd stages (1, 3, 5, 7)
Physical capacity is limited, scaling, or in flux. Signal Integrity is stronger — drivers and dispatchers are more transparent about real conditions. Stage 5 is the last recovery window before irreversible degradation.
Stage 9 — Exit / Reset: Pressure neutralized. Both conditions acknowledged. Plan replacement now.
The only stage where Asset Density and Signal Integrity limits are both fully acknowledged. No hidden tension. Carrier is transparent about exit or restructure. Rare exception: signals a breakthrough reset carrier rebuilding with full operational clarity.

Core metric definitions
Axiom Yield Score (AYS)
Predictive Load Acceptance Rating
A composite 0–100 carrier reliability score calculated from four operating inputs: HOS availability, route volatility, Signal Integrity, and recovery debt. Higher scores indicate safer dispatch windows. Scores below 40 trigger Stage 8 or Stage 9 flags — do not dispatch under any conditions.
Asset Density
SAP: P.Stable · Industry: Equipment / Driver Availability
The physical capacity of a carrier at the current moment — trucks available, HOS banked, active authority, equipment on the road. High Asset Density means the carrier has the physical infrastructure to execute the load. Critically: Stage 8 carriers have high Asset Density while concealing maximum failure risk. Asset Density alone is never sufficient for dispatch decisions.
Signal Integrity
SAP: C.Stable · Industry: Dispatch Responsiveness
The honesty, accuracy, and reliability of communication from the driver or dispatcher. High Signal Integrity means check calls are accurate, ETAs are realistic, and problems are disclosed proactively. Low Signal Integrity — dispatchers going quiet, ETAs inflating, load status unclear — is the primary failure predictor regardless of surface capacity metrics. Signal Integrity degrading while Asset Density stays high is the Stage 8 early warning signature.
Blind Spot Metric
SAP: Inversion Principle · Industry: Hidden Risk Indicator
The core detection mechanism behind Stage 8 identification. When Asset Density is at its highest, Signal Integrity pressure accumulates invisibly underneath. A carrier's surface compliance score is inversely correlated with their true failure risk at Stage 8. This is what load boards cannot see — and the reason Luminark Logistics Overwatch exists. Good metrics masking bad operations.
Recovery Debt
Failure Probability Trending Up
Accumulated operational fatigue measured in days since the carrier's last recovery run — a short-haul load with guaranteed home time or extended rest. High recovery debt is the primary driver of Stage 8 failure events: breakdowns, missed deliveries, and sudden carrier dropout. A carrier running 12+ days without a recovery window is approaching Stage 8 regardless of their current HOS number.
Entropy Load
Risk Score Increase / Failure Probability Trending Up
The aggregate operational pressure from route difficulty: weather severity, traffic density, dock delays, and congestion. High entropy load accelerates stage progression — pushing carriers toward Stage 8 faster than recovery debt alone. A carrier in Stage 4 assigned three consecutive high-entropy routes without a recovery run can reach Stage 8 within a single dispatch week.
Ghost Score
Operating Pattern Analysis / Fraud Detection Algorithm
Composite fraud detection score derived from MC authority age, load volume variance, and contact anomaly index. Scores below 35 confirm a synthetic carrier operation — no authentic growth history, unlimited lane availability, and a recently issued authority number with no verifiable freight activity. Primary indicator: absence of legitimate operational variance.
Lane Pressure Index
Market Tightness Index / Corridor Capacity Tension
Corridor-level capacity risk score tracking demand volume against available carrier supply on a given freight lane. Used to set rate multipliers 24–48 hours before market-visible capacity shortages. Stage 8 lanes warrant 1.15x–1.25x base rate — booked before the board reflects the shortage.

What this is

Luminark Logistics Overwatch is a predictive carrier risk platform built on the Stanfield Axiom of Perpetuity (SAP) — a proprietary 10-stage operational risk framework developed by Meridian Axiom Alignment Technologies. Standard load board tools show you historical compliance data. Overwatch shows you where a carrier is going — scoring current operating conditions against a predictive failure model.

The platform identifies Stage 8 carriers — those who appear fully compliant but are 24–72 hours from a service failure — before the load is tendered. It also screens for ghost carrier MC patterns, tracks lane-level capacity pressure ahead of market visibility, and delivers dispatch-ready guidance for every risk stage.

⊛ Axiom Yield Score

0–100 predictive reliability score from four dispatch metrics. Replaces gut-feel carrier selection with a repeatable, data-driven protocol. Any score below 40 is a do-not-dispatch signal regardless of load board history.

◎ Ghost Carrier Filter

MC number screening against synthetic carrier patterns. The filter scores the absence of authentic operational variance as the primary fraud signal — unlimited availability with no capacity constraints is the tell.

⊞ Lane Pressure Intelligence

Corridor-level capacity forecasting 24–48 hours ahead of board visibility. Stage 8 lanes get a 1.15x–1.25x rate multiplier recommendation before the shortage is visible. Quote now. Negotiate later.
