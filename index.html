Ghost Carrier Detection Filter
Fraudulent MC pattern screening — Operating Pattern Analysis algorithm
How the detection algorithm works

Legitimate carriers build operational history through natural growth cycles — high volume periods followed by capacity contraction, route changes, and recovery. This creates measurable variance. Fraudulent carriers show none of it. Their MC history is artificially clean: recently issued authority, zero load variance, suspiciously high contact responsiveness across every lane. The Ghost Filter scores the absence of authentic operational variance as the primary fraud signal.

Ghost Score = (authority_age × 0.30) + (volume_variance × 0.40) + (contact_anomaly × 0.30)
authority_age = min(carrier_age_yrs × 20, 100) · volume_variance = normalized load history variance
contact_anomaly = (10 − contact_quality) × 10 · Flag threshold: score < 35 → Ghost pattern confirmed
Primary signal: A carrier available on every lane, answering on the first ring, with zero capacity constraints is a red flag — not a green one. Unlimited availability = synthetic operation. Legitimate owner-operators have defined capacity windows.

The Blind Spot Metric — why perfect scorecards mask real risk
High Asset Density — Even stages (2, 4, 6, 8)
Fleet capacity present and bookable. HOS banked. Equipment available. Surface metrics look strong. Stage 4 is the sweet spot. Stage 8 has the identical surface profile — with maximum failure risk underneath. Asset Density alone is never sufficient for dispatch decisions.
Constrained Capacity — Odd stages (1, 3, 5, 7)
Physical capacity is limited, scaling, or in flux. Signal Integrity is typically stronger — drivers and dispatchers are more transparent about real conditions. Stage 5 is the last recovery window before irreversible degradation.
Stage 9 — Exit / Reset: Pressure neutralized. Both limits acknowledged. Plan replacement immediately.
The only stage where Asset Density and Signal Integrity limits are both fully transparent. No hidden tension. Carrier is honest about exit or restructure. Rare exception: signals a breakthrough reset carrier rebuilding with full operational clarity.

SAP Glossary — Freight & Logistics Edition
Industry translation of every term used in Luminark Logistics Overwatch
Core metric definitions
Axiom Yield Score (AYS)
Predictive Load Acceptance Rating
A composite 0–100 carrier reliability score from four operating inputs: HOS availability, route volatility, Signal Integrity, and recovery debt. Any score below 40 is a do-not-dispatch signal regardless of load board history.
Asset Density
SAP: P.Stable · Equipment / Driver Availability
The physical capacity of a carrier at the current moment — trucks available, HOS banked, active authority, equipment on the road. Critically: Stage 8 carriers have maximum Asset Density while concealing maximum failure risk underneath. Asset Density alone never justifies a dispatch decision.
Signal Integrity
SAP: C.Stable · Dispatch Responsiveness
The honesty, accuracy, and reliability of communication from driver or dispatcher. High Signal Integrity: check calls accurate, ETAs realistic, problems disclosed proactively. Signal Integrity degrading while Asset Density stays high is the Stage 8 early warning signature. Use the 📞 Signal Check tool to score this during every vetting call.
Blind Spot Metric
Hidden Risk Indicator · Good Metrics Masking Bad Operations
The core Stage 8 detection mechanism. When Asset Density is highest, Signal Integrity pressure accumulates invisibly underneath. A carrier's surface compliance score is inversely correlated with true failure risk at Stage 8. Good metrics masking bad operations — what load boards cannot see.
Recovery Debt
Failure Probability Trending Up
Accumulated operational fatigue measured in days since the carrier's last recovery run — short haul with guaranteed home time or extended rest. A carrier running 12+ days without recovery is approaching Stage 8 regardless of current HOS number. High recovery debt is the primary driver of Stage 8 failure events.
System Rigidity Flag
Eye of the Storm Trigger
Triggered when a carrier shows high Asset Density (strong HOS, clean metrics) combined with 14+ days without a recovery run. The calculator surfaces this automatically. A truck running perfectly for two weeks with no rest window is a ticking time bomb — the rigidity flag fires before Stage 8 is formally reached.
Ghost Score
Operating Pattern Analysis / Fraud Detection
Composite fraud score from MC authority age, load volume variance, and contact anomaly index. Scores below 35 confirm a synthetic carrier — no authentic growth history, unlimited availability, recently issued authority with no verifiable freight activity. Primary signal: absence of legitimate operational variance.
Lane Pressure Index
Market Tightness Index / Corridor Capacity Tension
Corridor-level capacity risk score tracking demand volume against available carrier supply. Used to set rate multipliers 24–48 hours before market-visible shortages. Stage 8 lanes warrant 1.15x–1.25x base rate — booked before the board reflects the shortage.
